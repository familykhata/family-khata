Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 30 July 2026

Welcome to Family Khata. Family Khata is a secure family finance management application designed to manage income, expenses, members and reports. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Information We Collect

  • Gmail Address
  • Display Name
  • Family Name
  • Family Members
  • Member Roles
  • Income Records
  • Expense Records
  • Khata Information
  • Notes entered by users
  • Encrypted Security PIN

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Bank Passwords
  • Credit Card Numbers
  • Debit Card Numbers
  • UPI PIN
  • Aadhaar Number
  • PAN Number

3. Why We Collect Information

  • Create Family Account
  • Verify Gmail OTP
  • Authenticate Login
  • Maintain Family Ledger
  • Generate Reports
  • Improve App Performance
  • Provide Customer Support

4. Firebase Services

Family Khata uses Google Firebase including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage (Future)

Google Privacy Policy

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Storage

All data is securely stored inside Google Firebase Cloud Firestore.

6. Security

  • HTTPS Encryption
  • Firebase Security Rules
  • Google Authentication
  • Security PIN Protection
  • Firestore Access Control

7. Data Sharing

We never sell or rent your personal information. Data is only shared when legally required or necessary to provide Firebase services.

8. Data Retention

Your data remains stored while your account remains active.

9. Delete Your Data

You may request deletion of your Family Khata data by contacting us.

10. Children's Privacy

Family Khata is intended for family financial management and is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever necessary.
