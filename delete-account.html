Delete Your Family Khata Account

If you wish to permanently delete your Family Khata account and associated data, you may request deletion by contacting us.

Email: funwithmewal@gmail.com

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to the support email address.
  2. Use the subject: Delete My Family Khata Account
  3. Include your registered Gmail address.
  4. Our team will verify your request.
  5. Your account will be permanently deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Family Account
  • Family Members
  • Income Records
  • Expense Records
  • Khata Records
  • Reports
  • Security PIN
  • Login History

Data Retention

Certain records may be retained only if required by applicable law or to resolve security, fraud prevention, or legal obligations.

Deletion Time

Verified deletion requests are normally processed within 7 working days.
