About Family Khata

Family Khata is a modern finance management application designed for families to maintain income, expenses, members and financial reports in one secure place.

Features

💰 Income & Expense

Track all income and expense records with ease.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Members

Manage family members with secure access.

📊 Reports

Generate daily, monthly and custom reports.

🔒 Secure Login

Google Sign-In / Gmail OTP and secure PIN protection.

☁ Cloud Sync

Secure synchronization using Firebase Cloud Firestore.

📱 Easy to Use

Simple interface designed for every family member.
